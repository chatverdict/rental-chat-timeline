Rental Chat Timeline

Need to show when you asked your landlord (or tenant) about a repair, and what they answered? Load the exported chat and get a dated list of the lines that look like repair or maintenance requests, replies and promises, rent and deposit mentions, and notices or deadlines. Free. The chat file is read in your browser and its contents are never sent anywhere. The page only reports anonymous visit and "a file was processed" counts (no text, names or file data).
